Controls
WASD: movement; Ctrl: slow; Shift: fast; Space: run (donor bindings).
E: enter/exit nearest real Alien ventilation opening; Q: exit.
Left mouse: attack an eligible survivor; G: roar; V: change view.
Mouse wheel: distance; Shift+wheel: height; Ctrl+wheel: shoulder offset.
Middle mouse: reset third-person camera parameters.
